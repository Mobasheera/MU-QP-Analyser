    The document pipeline

    01

    Extract

    PyMuPDF pulls native text where it exists; pages with no usable text layer fall back to OpenCV-cleaned, deskewed OCR.

    02

    Segment

    Every Q1(a), Q2(b)... sub-part becomes its own question — never one blob of PDF text.

    03

    Analyze

    Twelve NLP stages run per question and across the corpus: tokenization through topic clustering.

    04

    Reveal

    Topic frequency, repeated questions, and semantic search — rendered on a single dashboard.

    What you'll get on the dashboard

    Topic Detection

    Questions clustered by embedding similarity and labeled with the syllabus topic they belong to.

    Repeated Questions

    Semantically near-identical questions across different years, with a similarity score.

    Word Cloud & N-Grams

    The vocabulary and phrase patterns (bigrams/trigrams) that dominate the corpus.

    Concept Search

    Search by keyword or by meaning — "stemming" also surfaces "Porter's algorithm" questions.
